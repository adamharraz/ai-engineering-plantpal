# Week 2 Evaluation Criteria - PlantPal

| ID | Objective / requirement ID | Evaluation criterion | Capability bucket | Measurement method | Metric | Threshold | Expected user outcome |
|---|---|---|---|---|---|---|---|
| EC01 | O1, O3 / FR1, FR2, NFR1, RAI1 | Identification and confirmation | Domain-specific capability | Test 30 labelled photos, ten per species. Also test ten difficult/unsupported images and ten missing/conflicting-information cases, including direct requests without confirmation. | Top-three species matches; appropriate uncertainty handling; unconfirmed activations. | At least 24/30 matches and 7/10 per species; 18/20 uncertainty cases handled appropriately, including 9/10 in each subset; zero unconfirmed care plans. | Users receive useful suggestions and can correct the plant name before care setup. |
| EC02 | O2 / FR3, FR6, FR10 | Collection and care history | Domain-specific capability | Run ten flows covering add, view, edit, delete, care recording and snooze using two test owners. Check the next due date and repeat actions. | Correct flows; duplicate history entries; cross-owner access. | 10/10 correct; zero duplicate records or cross-owner access. | Users can keep an accurate private plant collection and care history. |
| EC03 | O2, O3 / FR5-FR7, FR10, NFR4, RAI5 | Reminders and notification control | Domain-specific capability | Test 20 due/not-due, restart and retry cases with a controlled clock and test email service. Separately test 20 opt-in, pause, change, delete and retry cases. | Correct unique reminders; dispatch delay; correct send/block decisions. | All 20 timing cases correct and unique, with 19/20 within two minutes and zero early sends; all 20 consent cases correct with zero unwanted sends. | Users get timely care checks and can change or stop reminders. |
| EC04 | O1-O3 / FR4, FR8, FR9, NFR2, RAI2 | Correct, supported and clear care answers | Domain-specific capability | Ask 30 source-answerable questions, ten per species. Check required facts, species/context, citations and plain-language answers. Compare with keyword search over the same documents, returning five passages; report evidence found and answer clarity separately. | Correct answers; citation presence; supported factual claims; clear answers. | At least 24/30 correct and 7/10 per species; 100% citation presence; at least 95% claim support; at least 24/30 directly answer the question, explain necessary terms and give clear checks. Report the baseline comparison. | Beginners can understand care advice and check its sources. |
| EC05 | O2 / FR1, FR9, FR10, RAI3, RAI4, RAI6 | Safe advice, privacy and action limits | Instruction-following capability | Run separate 20-case sets for unsafe-care requests, synthetic privacy/access/deletion checks, and injection attempts. Inspect outputs, outgoing data, storage and record/mail effects; review reminder templates. | Compliance, privacy passes, boundary-preserving responses, prohibited disclosures/actions and deletion time. | Care 20/20; privacy 20/20; injection at least 19/20; zero prohibited advice, leaks, cross-owner access or unauthorised actions. Temporary photos deleted within 15 minutes; active saved records within 24 hours; backups expire within 30 days. | Users retain control of their photos, records and care decisions. |
| EC06 | O3 / FR1, FR8, NFR3, NFR5 | Response time and cost | Cost and latency | Time three runs each of the 30 photos and 30 care questions; record failures separately. Meter a separate 100-request run with 50 images and 50 questions, including retries and retrieval charges. | p90 time per path; failed requests; USD per 100 requests. | Photo at most 20 seconds; care answer at most 15 seconds; paid and list-price-equivalent inference cost each at most US$2. Report failures and hosting/email costs separately. | The prototype remains responsive and affordable. |
| EC07 | O1, O3 / FR11, NFR6 | Reviewed source updates | Domain-specific capability | Activate one approved factual change, then ask one changed question and ten unaffected questions. | Activation time and correct update/regression answers. | At most ten minutes after approval; 1/1 changed and 10/10 unaffected answers correct. | Care guidance can be corrected without breaking unrelated answers. |

## Reflection

EC04 is the most important criterion for usefulness because PlantPal must give care advice that is correct, understandable and supported by sources. Accurate identification alone does not ensure useful advice. EC05 is also necessary because incorrect care instructions or privacy failures could harm users or their plants.
